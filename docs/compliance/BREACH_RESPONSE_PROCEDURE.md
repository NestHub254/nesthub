# NestHub / SIONA data breach response procedure

**Status:** DRAFT — internal  
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

## 1. Detect and contain

1. Record time detected, systems affected, and initial impact.  
2. Contain: rotate credentials SIONA controls, revoke excess access, preserve logs.  
3. Do not destroy evidence.

## 2. Classify

Classify whether the incident involves personal data and whether NestHub product records, SIONA infrastructure records, or both are affected.

## 3. Escalate

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| SIONA on-call / ops | Containment and technical investigation |
| NestHub product contact | Customer / user communications for NestHub product impact |
| Privacy lead (interim: NestHub mailbox + SIONA compliance owner) | Legal assessment, ODPC notification decision |

## 4. Notify

Follow applicable law (including Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 requirements where triggered).  
Customer notices for NestHub product incidents are led by NestHub unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 5. Remediate and record

Document root cause, corrective actions, and lessons learned in the internal compliance register.
