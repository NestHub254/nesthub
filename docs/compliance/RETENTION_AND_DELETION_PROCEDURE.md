# Data retention and deletion procedure

**Status:** DRAFT — internal  
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

## Retention

| Record class | Primary owner | Interim retention approach |
|---|---|---|
| NestHub product / tenant / property records | NestHub | NestHub determines periods; SIONA does not invent SLAs |
| Website enquiry copies (e.g. Formspree) | NestHub | NestHub confirms processor retention |
| Hosting / security logs | SIONA | Keep only as needed for security, ops, troubleshooting, legal duty |
| Privacy request tickets | SIONA + NestHub | Keep as needed to evidence fulfilment |

## Deletion

Follow the public `/data-deletion/` seven-step procedure.  
Do not promise automated deletion.  
Distinguish account deletion from statutory retention holds.
