NestHub Privacy policy

Data Deletion

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Disclosure only. This page explains how deletion requests are handled under the NestHub × SIONA technology partnership (Option A). NestHub does not publish an automated account-deletion API on this website. Submitting a request does not itself delete records. SIONA cannot delete information it cannot access or control.

1. Option A workflow

  1. Submit a request through NestHub’s existing contact channel: email hello@nesthub.app (subject “NestHub data deletion request”), or call / WhatsApp +254 701 388 400 and follow up in writing. Include the email used for your NestHub account or enquiry, your full name, your role (owner, staff, tenant, or website enquiry contact), a clear deletion statement, and any property or unit references that help locate records.
  2. Identity is verified using reasonable steps (for example confirming control of the registered email).
  3. The relevant controller is identified for each class of record (for example the property operator for tenant/property workspace data they control, the NestHub operating entity for NestHub-operator records, or SIONA for SIONA-controlled corporate/infrastructure records).
  4. SIONA records and coordinates the request as part of privacy administration under the technology partnership.
  5. Where SIONA acts as processor, the relevant controller provides the necessary documented instructions.
  6. SIONA executes authorised actions on systems it operates (for example hosting artefacts or technical records under its control).
  7. Deletion, anonymisation or lawful retention is documented, including any statutory or contractual hold.
  8. The requester receives an appropriate response describing the outcome at a high level, without exposing other people’s personal data.

2. Record classes (distinguished)

  • NestHub account deletion — platform sign-in identity and related NestHub-operator account records, where NestHub controls them.
  • Property-owner workspace records — operational content controlled by the property owner / property-management business in its workspace.
  • Tenant personal information — typically controlled by the relevant property operator; SIONA processes under instructions when acting as processor.
  • Infrastructure and security logs — technical records on systems SIONA operates; retained or deleted according to security and legal need.
  • Statutory and contractual retention — may prevent full deletion even after an account or workspace request.

3. What cannot be promised

  • Automatic or self-service wipe of all NestHub systems.
  • Deletion of third-party processor copies that NestHub or a property operator must request separately (for example Formspree enquiry copies, if used).
  • Deletion of records outside systems SIONA operates or is authorised to access.
