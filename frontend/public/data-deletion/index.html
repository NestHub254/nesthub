NestHub Privacy policy

Data Deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information related to NestHub. Submitting a request does not automatically delete an account or related records.

How to request deletion

  1. Submit a request
    Email hello@nesthub.app or call / WhatsApp +254 701 388 400. Please include your name, the email address associated with your NestHub account or enquiry, and a clear description of what you would like deleted.
  2. Identity verification
    Reasonable steps may be taken to verify your identity before acting on a request.
  3. Property workspace information
    Where a request concerns tenant or property records held in a property workspace, you may be directed to the relevant property owner or property manager, who is responsible for that information.
  4. Review
    The responsible organisation reviews the request under applicable data protection requirements.
  5. Outcome
    You will receive an appropriate response. Instant deletion is not guaranteed, and NestHub does not provide an automated deletion service on this website.
  6. Lawful retention
    Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate legal obligations. Records outside the responsible organisation’s control cannot be deleted through this process alone.
