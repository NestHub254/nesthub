Data Deletion

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · NestHub product requests are handled by NestHub · SIONA supports technology-infrastructure deletion where applicable

How this page works. This page explains how to request deletion. It is documentation and disclosure only. It does not itself delete accounts or database records, and NestHub does not publish an account-deletion API on this website.

1. How to request deletion

Email hello@nesthub.app with the subject line “NestHub data deletion request” and include:

  • the email address used for your NestHub account or enquiry;
  • your full name;
  • whether you are an owner, staff user, tenant, or website enquiry contact;
  • a clear statement that you want your personal data deleted;
  • any property or unit references that help locate your records.

You may also call or WhatsApp NestHub on +254 701 388 400 and ask to follow up in writing by email so there is an auditable request trail.

2. Identity verification

Before NestHub acts on a deletion request, NestHub will take reasonable steps to verify that the requester controls the relevant account or enquiry identity. Verification may include confirming access to the registered email address or asking for limited additional information already associated with the account. NestHub will not ask for unnecessary documents.

3. What NestHub handles

NestHub (and its operating partner) is responsible for NestHub product records such as user accounts, property associations, and any NestHub-managed tenant or property operational data stored in NestHub systems. Requests about landlord/tenant business records that NestHub manages should be directed to NestHub using the contact details above.

4. What SIONA Technologies Limited handles

SIONA Technologies Limited is the technology partner. Where a verified deletion request affects infrastructure artefacts that SIONA controls — for example hosting logs, deployment diagnostics, or technical support records tied to NestHub infrastructure — SIONA processes deletion or anonymisation within that technology-provider scope after NestHub confirms the request. SIONA does not independently operate NestHub customer support for property or tenant account deletion.

5. Legal retention

Some information may need to be retained where NestHub or SIONA has a verified legal obligation, dispute, security, or accounting requirement. If part of a request cannot be completed for that reason, NestHub should explain what was retained and why.

6. Confirmation

After NestHub completes the NestHub-side request (and any coordinated SIONA infrastructure steps), NestHub should send email confirmation to the requester describing the outcome at a high level. Confirmation will not expose other people’s personal data.
