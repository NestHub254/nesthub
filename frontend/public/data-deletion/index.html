Request data deletion

Use these steps to ask NestHub or a property manager to review information associated with your account or enquiry.

Updated 23 November 2026

Request guide

Choose the right contactSend the requestReview and responseRecords that may remain

1. Choose the right contact

Tenant or tenancy records: Contact the property owner or manager who created your NestHub account. They control the tenancy information in their workspace and can review the request.

NestHub account or website enquiry: Contact the NestHub team by phone or WhatsApp at +254 701 388 400. Include “NestHub data request” so it can be routed correctly.

2. Send the request

Include only the information needed to locate the record:

  • your name;
  • the email address or phone number associated with your NestHub account or enquiry;
  • whether the request concerns a NestHub account, a website enquiry, or property workspace records; and
  • what you want deleted or reviewed.
Protect your account. Do not include your password, one-time code, identity document, full bank details, or full payment credentials in an initial message. The team will explain if additional verification is needed and how to provide it safely.

3. Review and response

The responsible team may ask for reasonable information to confirm identity and locate the relevant records. It will review the request under applicable data protection requirements and explain the outcome or any further steps.

Submitting a request does not automatically erase an account or workspace record. The website does not currently provide a self-service deletion button.

4. Records that may need to remain

Some records may be retained where necessary for a legal obligation, an active dispute, security, or the property manager’s legitimate recordkeeping needs. If the request concerns information controlled by a property manager, NestHub may direct the request to that organisation.

For details about information handling, read the Privacy Policy. The Terms of Service explain the rules for using NestHub.

Need help with a request?

Call or WhatsApp +254 701 388 400. Do not share account passwords or sign-in codes.
