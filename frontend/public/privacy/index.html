Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. About NestHub

NestHub is a property management platform designed to help property owners and property management businesses manage their operations.

NestHub works with SIONA Technologies Limited as its technology partner.

2. Personal information we may collect

Depending on how NestHub is used, personal information may include:

  • names, email addresses and telephone numbers;
  • account and login details;
  • roles and workspace associations (for example owner, staff or tenant);
  • property and unit references entered into a workspace;
  • messages or enquiry details submitted through NestHub contact channels; and
  • technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device or connection details.

3. Why information is used

Personal information may be used to:

  • provide NestHub accounts and workspaces;
  • support property and tenant management features;
  • respond to enquiries and support requests;
  • operate, maintain and secure the platform; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

Where required under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, processing is based on one or more lawful grounds such as consent, performance of a contract, a legal obligation, or other grounds permitted by law.

4. Responsibilities for workspace information

When customers use NestHub to manage tenant and property information, they are responsible for determining the purposes for which that information is collected and used, subject to applicable law.

Technology service providers may process personal information as necessary to deliver and maintain authorised NestHub services, in accordance with applicable agreements and instructions.

For questions about information held within a particular property workspace, tenants should first contact the relevant property owner or property manager.

5. How information is protected

NestHub and its technology partners take appropriate measures to protect personal information and limit access to authorised parties. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law or legitimate legal obligations. Retention needs may differ depending on the type of record involved.

7. Your rights

Subject to the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and other applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to ask questions about how your information is handled.

To exercise these rights, use the contact details below. Deletion requests are also described on the Data Deletion page.

8. Service providers and transfers

NestHub uses trusted technology service providers to host and support the platform. Those providers may process personal information only as needed to provide their services.

Where personal information is processed outside Kenya, appropriate safeguards are applied in line with applicable law.
