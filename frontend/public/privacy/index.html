2. Roles: controller and processor

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, a data controller determines the purpose and means of processing. A data processor processes personal data on behalf of a controller.

SIONA Technologies Limited is registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as both a Data Controller and a Data Processor (Identification: 964-900E-BFFD; validity shown on the certificates: 10 November 2026 – 10 November 2028).

That dual registration does not mean SIONA is the controller of every landlord’s or tenant’s personal information processed through NestHub. In practice:

  • SIONA as controller — for personal data SIONA determines the purpose of processing itself (for example SIONA’s own corporate operations, infrastructure security telemetry it determines how to use, and partnership administration contacts it holds as SIONA).
  • SIONA as processor — when SIONA handles NestHub-related personal information on behalf of NestHub and/or property owners or other controllers, according to documented instructions and the technology partnership.
  • NestHub / property operators — remain responsible for NestHub product, customer and property-related privacy decisions for records they control. Other controllers or processors may exist under separate arrangements; this policy does not invent those roles.

SIONA does not claim ownership of all NestHub data and is not the sole controller of all tenant information.

3. Scope

This policy covers personal information that may be processed in connection with:

  • the NestHub public website and contact / demo forms;
  • NestHub account registration and sign-in;
  • the NestHub staff / owner application and tenant portal;
  • hosting, security, logging, privacy administration and support activities performed under the technology partnership.

It does not cover third-party websites linked from NestHub, or independent NestHub partner business systems outside the NestHub application.

4. Categories of personal data

4.1 NestHub application account and workspace data

Based on the NestHub application design, account and workspace data may include:

  • identity and contact details such as full name, email address, and phone number;
  • authentication credentials stored as a password hash (not the plain-text password);
  • role information (for example owner, manager / reception, maintenance, or tenant);
  • property association details such as property identifiers, property name, address, and unit label;
  • technical session information required to keep a user signed in.

4.2 Product capabilities that may process additional data when enabled

The NestHub product design also contemplates leases, payments, maintenance tickets, announcements, and staff messages. This policy does not claim those datasets are live in production unless NestHub has enabled the corresponding services.

4.3 Website enquiry data

The public website demo / contact form may collect name, email, phone, property name, and message content. The form endpoint configured in the current website build uses Formspree.

4.4 Technology-partner operational data

SIONA may process limited technical and compliance-support data such as request logs, IP addresses, security diagnostics, build/deploy metadata, privacy-request administration records, and support correspondence related to NestHub infrastructure.

5. Purposes of processing

  • to provide NestHub accounts, workspaces, and role-based access;
  • to respond to demo or contact requests;
  • to operate, secure, monitor and improve hosting and application infrastructure;
  • to administer privacy, deletion and compliance requests under the technology partnership;
  • to meet legal obligations applicable to NestHub or to SIONA in its controller or processor capacity;
  • to handle security incidents and support requests.

6. Lawful bases

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may rely on one or more of the following bases under the Data Protection Act, 2019 (and any other applicable law): consent; performance of a contract; a legal obligation; legitimate interests that do not override the data subject’s interests (for example securing hosted systems); or another basis permitted by law. The specific basis for a NestHub product activity is determined by the relevant controller for that activity.

7. How information is processed and protected

NestHub is responsible for product-level access controls, customer onboarding, and day-to-day handling of NestHub user and property records it controls.

SIONA’s technology and compliance-support responsibilities include configuring hosting, protecting infrastructure credentials it controls, applying security headers and transport encryption for hosted web surfaces it manages, limiting staff access on a need-to-know basis, and supporting privacy administration consistent with its contractual role.

Passwords in the NestHub application design are stored as hashed values. HTTPS is used for production web access.

8. Who may receive or process information

  • NestHub operators and authorised NestHub staff;
  • SIONA Technologies Limited staff acting under the technology partnership;
  • infrastructure providers used to host NestHub (for example cloud hosting and related platform services);
  • form or messaging processors used by NestHub for website enquiries, if enabled (currently Formspree in the public site build);
  • professional advisers or authorities where legally required.

SIONA does not use NestHub customer data for SIONA’s own marketing.

9. International transfers

NestHub hosting and related cloud services may store or process data in regions selected for the deployment. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, the relevant controller and SIONA (as applicable) align safeguards with applicable law and the live hosting configuration.

10. Retention

Retention periods for NestHub customer, tenant, lease, payment and support records are determined by NestHub / its operating partner and any applicable legal or contractual retention duties.

SIONA retains infrastructure logs, technical records and privacy-administration records only as long as needed for security, operations, troubleshooting, compliance support and legal obligations applicable to the services it provides. Account deletion requests do not override statutory or lawful retention requirements where those apply.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or information about how your personal data is handled. How to exercise deletion rights is described on the Data Deletion page.

12. Access, correction and deletion requests

Send NestHub privacy requests to hello@nesthub.app. NestHub and SIONA will take reasonable steps to verify the requester’s identity before acting on account or property-related records. Where a request concerns technology infrastructure or compliance-support records controlled by SIONA, NestHub coordinates with SIONA according to SIONA’s role.

13. Security incidents and breach notification

If you believe there has been unauthorised access to NestHub personal data, contact hello@nesthub.app promptly. NestHub leads customer communication for NestHub product incidents. SIONA supports NestHub on infrastructure investigation and coordinates breach response steps for systems SIONA operates, including escalation consistent with applicable law and the technology partnership.

You may also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner or another competent authority.

14. Children

NestHub is intended for property-business and adult tenant use. It is not directed at children.
