2. How roles work (Option A)

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, a data controller determines the purpose and essential means of processing. A data processor processes personal data on behalf of a controller under documented instructions.

2.1 Property owners and property-management businesses

Where a property owner or property-management business uses its NestHub workspace to manage tenants, units, leases, maintenance and related property operations, that business generally determines the purposes and essential means of processing the tenant and property-related personal information it controls, and is the relevant controller for that information — subject to the actual contractual and operational arrangement for that workspace.

Not every NestHub user automatically becomes a controller for every category of data; the assessment depends on who decides why and how particular personal information is processed.

2.2 SIONA as processor

SIONA processes NestHub-related personal information as a data processor when it acts on documented instructions from the relevant controller — for example hosting, authorised access management, infrastructure security, operational data administration, privacy-request coordination and related technical support on systems SIONA operates.

2.3 SIONA as independent controller

SIONA also acts as an independent data controller for its own corporate, administrative, infrastructure and other processing activities where SIONA determines the purposes and means (for example SIONA staff records, partnership administration, and security telemetry SIONA decides how to use for protecting systems it operates).

2.4 NestHub operating entity

The NestHub operating entity remains responsible for its own independent processing activities (for example product administration, platform accounts it controls, and NestHub-led customer communications).

2.5 ODPC registration (SIONA)

SIONA Technologies Limited is registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as both a Data Controller (certificate serial 33476) and a Data Processor (certificate serial 33477) under Identification 964-900E-BFFD. Validity shown on the certificates: 10 November 2026 – 10 November 2028.

Dual registration does not make SIONA the controller of all NestHub personal information, and does not make SIONA the sole controller of all tenant information.

3. Scope

This policy covers personal information that may be processed in connection with:

  • the NestHub public website and contact / demo forms;
  • NestHub account registration and sign-in;
  • owner, staff and tenant workspaces;
  • hosting, security, logging, privacy administration and compliance coordination under the technology partnership.

It does not cover third-party websites linked from NestHub, or independent business systems outside NestHub.

4. Types of personal information

  • Identity and contact details (name, email, phone).
  • Account credentials stored as a password hash (not plain-text passwords).
  • Role and workspace association (owner, staff, tenant, property/unit labels).
  • Property-related operational content entered into NestHub when those features are used (for example lease, maintenance or payment-related fields contemplated by the product design).
  • Website enquiry content submitted through the public demo/contact form (Formspree endpoint in the current build).
  • Technical and security data such as IP addresses, request logs, session tokens and deploy/diagnostics metadata.

This policy does not claim that every product capability is live in production unless NestHub has enabled it.

5. Processing purposes

  • Provide NestHub accounts, workspaces and role-based access.
  • Enable property operators to manage tenant and property operations they control.
  • Respond to demo or contact requests.
  • Host, secure, monitor and administer NestHub infrastructure.
  • Coordinate privacy, deletion and compliance requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Investigate and respond to security incidents.

6. Lawful bases

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may rely on consent; performance of a contract; a legal obligation; legitimate interests that do not override the data subject’s interests (for example securing hosted systems); or another basis permitted by the Data Protection Act, 2019. The relevant controller for each activity is responsible for identifying the applicable basis.

7. Security safeguards

Measures applied on systems SIONA operates include HTTPS for production web access, Hosting security headers, access limited on a need-to-know basis, and protection of infrastructure credentials SIONA controls. NestHub product-level access controls remain the NestHub operator’s and workspace administrators’ responsibility. No certification or absolute security guarantee is claimed.

8. Third-party service providers

  • Google Cloud / Firebase Hosting for the public NestHub website.
  • Formspree for the public demo/contact form endpoint configured in the current website build.
  • DNS and related edge services used for the NestHub hostname.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where legally required.

SIONA does not use NestHub customer data for SIONA’s own marketing.

9. International transfers

Hosting and related cloud services may process data in regions selected for the deployment. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, the relevant controller and SIONA (as applicable) align safeguards with applicable law and the live hosting configuration.

10. Retention

Retention for tenant and property records controlled by a property operator is determined by that controller and applicable legal or contractual duties. NestHub-operator records follow NestHub’s own requirements. SIONA retains infrastructure logs, technical records and privacy-administration records only as long as needed for security, operations, troubleshooting, compliance support and legal obligations applicable to services it provides. Specific retention periods are not invented in this policy.

11. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or information about how your personal data is handled. Deletion requests are described on the Data Deletion page.

12. Data deletion

Requests are coordinated under Option A: identity is verified, the relevant controller is identified, SIONA records and coordinates the request, processor actions follow documented instructions, and deletion, anonymisation or lawful retention is documented before a response is sent. Automated deletion APIs are not claimed on this website.

13. Breach escalation

If you believe there has been unauthorised access to NestHub personal data, contact hello@nesthub.app promptly. NestHub leads product-user communications for NestHub product incidents. SIONA supports investigation and containment for systems it operates and coordinates escalation consistent with applicable law and documented arrangements. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

14. Children

NestHub is intended for property-business and adult tenant use. It is not directed at children.
