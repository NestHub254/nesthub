Privacy Policy

Draft for production review · Effective date to be confirmed by NestHub before publication as final · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Status. This is a shared NestHub privacy policy draft prepared for production approval. NestHub (and its operating partner) remains responsible for NestHub product, customer, and property-related privacy decisions. SIONA Technologies Limited is the technology partner and contributes only the technology-provider sections verified below. Partner-specific legal details still pending confirmation are tracked internally and are not invented here.

1. Who we are

NestHub is a property and rental management software product published at https://nesthub.sionaglobal.com/. Public NestHub contacts currently shown on the website include hello@nesthub.app and telephone / WhatsApp +254 701 388 400.

SIONA Technologies Limited is the technology partner supporting hosting, technical infrastructure, and related engineering for NestHub. SIONA does not own NestHub’s brand, customer relationships, or partner property data. More about SIONA: https://sionaglobal.com.

This policy does not automatically treat the parties as joint controllers. Exact controller / processor roles for each processing activity will be confirmed in the NestHub–SIONA commercial and data-processing arrangements before this draft is marked final.

2. Scope

This policy covers personal information that may be processed in connection with:

  • the NestHub public website and contact / demo forms;
  • NestHub account registration and sign-in;
  • the NestHub staff / owner application and tenant portal;
  • hosting, security, logging, and support activities performed by the technology partner.

It does not cover third-party websites linked from NestHub, or independent NestHub partner business systems outside the NestHub application.

3. Categories of personal data

3.1 Verified in the current NestHub application design

Based on the NestHub codebase reviewed for this draft, account and workspace data may include:

  • identity and contact details such as full name, email address, and phone number;
  • authentication credentials stored as a password hash (not the plain-text password);
  • role information (for example owner, manager / reception, maintenance, or tenant);
  • property association details such as property identifiers, property name, address, and unit label;
  • technical session information required to keep a user signed in (for example authentication tokens held by the browser client).

3.2 Designed in the product schema but not claimed as live production processing here

The NestHub database design also contemplates leases, payments, maintenance tickets, announcements, and staff messages. Those categories are described as product capabilities. This policy does not claim that payment, lease, or other operational datasets are currently processed through a live production backend unless NestHub confirms that the corresponding services are enabled in the production environment.

3.3 Website enquiry data

The public website demo / contact form may collect name, email, phone, property name, and message content. The form endpoint configured in the current website build uses Formspree. NestHub should confirm whether that processor remains approved for production use.

3.4 Technology-partner operational data

SIONA may process limited technical data required to host and secure NestHub, such as request logs, IP addresses, security diagnostics, build/deploy metadata, and support correspondence related to infrastructure.

4. Why information is collected

  • to provide NestHub accounts, workspaces, and role-based access;
  • to respond to demo or contact requests;
  • to operate, secure, monitor, and improve hosting and application infrastructure;
  • to meet legal obligations that apply to NestHub or, where applicable, to SIONA as technology provider;
  • to handle privacy, security, and support requests.

5. How information is processed and protected

NestHub is responsible for product-level access controls, customer onboarding, and day-to-day handling of NestHub user and property records.

SIONA’s verified technology-provider responsibilities include configuring hosting, protecting infrastructure credentials it controls, applying security headers and transport encryption for hosted web surfaces it manages, and limiting staff access to production systems on a need-to-know basis.

Passwords in the NestHub application design are stored as hashed values. HTTPS is expected for production web access.

6. Who may receive or process information

  • NestHub operators and authorised NestHub staff;
  • SIONA Technologies Limited staff acting as technology partner for hosting and technical support;
  • infrastructure providers used to host NestHub (for example cloud hosting and related platform services);
  • form or messaging processors used by NestHub for website enquiries, if enabled;
  • professional advisers or authorities where legally required.

SIONA does not use NestHub customer data for SIONA’s own marketing.

7. International transfers

NestHub hosting and related cloud services may store or process data in regions selected for the deployment. Exact production regions and transfer safeguards will be confirmed by NestHub and SIONA against the live production configuration before this policy is finalised.

8. Retention

Retention periods for NestHub customer, tenant, lease, payment, and support records are determined by NestHub / its operating partner and must be confirmed before final publication. SIONA retains infrastructure logs and technical records only as long as needed for security, operations, troubleshooting, and legal obligations applicable to the technology services it provides.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your personal data is handled. How to exercise deletion rights is described on the Data Deletion page.

10. Access, correction and deletion requests

Send NestHub privacy requests to hello@nesthub.app. NestHub will verify the requester’s identity before acting on account or property-related records. Where a request concerns technology infrastructure controlled by SIONA, NestHub coordinates with SIONA. Users may also review SIONA’s public company site at sionaglobal.com for technology-partner context; NestHub remains the primary contact for NestHub product requests.

11. Security incidents and complaints

If you believe there has been unauthorised access to NestHub personal data, contact hello@nesthub.app promptly. NestHub leads customer communication for NestHub incidents. SIONA supports NestHub on infrastructure investigation where the incident involves systems SIONA operates.

You may also have the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority. NestHub should confirm the primary supervisory contact details for final publication.

12. Children

NestHub is intended for property-business and adult tenant use. It is not directed at children.

13. Changes

NestHub may update this policy as the product, hosting arrangement, or legal requirements change. Material updates should be reflected on this page with a revised date.
