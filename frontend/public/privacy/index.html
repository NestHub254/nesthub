Privacy and personal information

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you use NestHub, how it supports the service, and how to ask about your information.

Updated 23 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

NestHub is a rental and property operations platform developed with SIONA Technologies Limited as its technology partner. For questions about this policy or the website, contact the NestHub team by phone or WhatsApp using the details below.

For information entered into a property workspace about a tenancy, rent, or a maintenance request, the property owner or manager who operates that workspace decides why the records are used and is normally the first contact for a tenant request. NestHub and its technology providers may handle those records to operate the service for that property.

2. Information handled

The information depends on the features used and may include:

  • account details such as name, email address, role, and login credentials;
  • property, unit, tenancy, and staff assignment details entered by a workspace;
  • rent amounts, due dates, payment references, and payment status recorded by the workspace;
  • maintenance reports, messages, notices, and related attachments or links;
  • demo or support enquiry details submitted through the website; and
  • basic technical information needed to keep the service available, prevent abuse, and diagnose faults.

Passwords are processed by the authentication service as password hashes; they are not stored in the application database as readable passwords. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides an identity token used to authenticate your account.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, show each user the records allowed by their role, operate property and tenant workflows, deliver requested notices, respond to enquiries, maintain the platform, and meet applicable legal obligations.

The website demo form is processed by Formspree so the NestHub team can receive and respond to demo enquiries. Account and workspace features use NestHub’s hosted application services.

4. Service providers and access

Information may be available to the workspace owner or manager and to staff they authorise. To host and operate NestHub, relevant data may also be processed by infrastructure and service providers, including Supabase, Firebase, Vercel, Google sign-in, Formspree, and the email provider configured for a workspace.

Providers receive information needed for their service and are expected to protect it under their applicable terms and safeguards. NestHub does not sell personal information.

5. Storage and security

NestHub uses access controls intended to restrict workspace records by account role and property. Browser storage is used to keep a signed-in session on the device. Keep your device and account credentials secure, and sign out when using a shared device.

Hosting or service providers may process information outside Kenya. Appropriate safeguards should apply to any transfer where required by applicable law. No internet service can promise that transmission or storage is completely risk-free.

6. Retention

Information is kept for as long as it is needed for the service, the property workspace, support, security, or legal obligations. Retention may vary by record type. A property owner or manager may also need to keep tenancy or payment records for their own operational or legal purposes.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may ask to be informed about, access, correct, object to, restrict, or request erasure or portability of personal information. Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 sets out data subject rights and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner provides guidance on exercising them.

If the information is held in a property workspace, contact the owner or manager first. To request deletion of NestHub account or website enquiry information, follow the data deletion instructions. A request may need identity verification, and some records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

You may also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya if you wish to raise a complaint.

8. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or to make a request, call or WhatsApp NestHub at +254 701 388 400. Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, or full payment credentials in a request.

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.

For questions about a specific tenancy, rent record, or repair request, contact the property manager who operates that NestHub workspace.
